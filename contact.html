FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first pickup.

How does free pickup and delivery work?

Book a window online or by phone, leave your bag at the door, and our concierge handles the rest. Delivery is free on all orders over $30 and typically returns within 24–48 hours.

What if I'm not home during pickup?

No problem. Leave your bag in a secure spot or with a doorman and note it in your booking. Every item is RFID-tagged the moment we collect it, so nothing goes missing.

Do you handle delicate fabrics like silk and cashmere?

Absolutely — it's our specialty. Our chief care specialist is a certified textile conservator, and every delicate item gets an individualized cleaning protocol before it touches any machine.

How is pricing calculated?

Wash & Fold is priced by weight ($2.50/lb). Dry cleaning and pressing are per item, with exact quotes visible before you confirm any order. Black Card members save 20% across the board.

Is there a satisfaction guarantee?

Yes. If anything isn't perfect, let us know within 48 hours and we'll re-clean it free of charge — no questions asked.
