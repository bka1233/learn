Who We Are

Born from a Simple
Obsession

In 2012, founder Elena Marchetti grew frustrated that no laundry service treated her favorite silk blouses with the care they deserved. She opened a tiny 400-square-foot shop with one rule: every garment leaves better than it arrived.

That rule became a philosophy. Today, LuxeWash operates a 12,000-square-foot atelier staffed by trained textile specialists, serving over 25,000 households and businesses across Central Texas.

We still sort every order by hand. We still inspect every seam before packing. And we still believe that laundry — done right — is a form of respect.

Elena Marchetti
Founder & CEO

Milestones

Our Journey So Far

From one storefront to a citywide obsession — a decade of growth, one garment at a time.

2012

The First Wash

LuxeWash opens its doors on Silk Avenue with a single machine and a handwritten price list.

2016

Doorstep Delivery

We launch free pickup and delivery across Austin — and never look back. Orders triple in a year.

2020

The Atelier

A 12,000 sq ft facility opens with RFID tracking, eco-solvent dry cleaning, and a bridal suite.

2025

25K & Counting

We surpass 25,000 happy clients and introduce the LuxeWash Black Card membership.
